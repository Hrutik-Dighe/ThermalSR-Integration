Core Capabilities

What the pipeline solves



01
Multi-Sensor Alignment
Geometrically co-registers high-resolution optical images with low-resolution TIR data using learned keypoint matching and subpixel homography estimation.
02
Cross-Modal Fusion
Cross-attention transformer fuses optical spatial features with thermal embeddings — selectively, preventing false texture transfer to thermally flat regions.
03
Guided Super-Resolution
Pixel-shuffle upsampling guided by optical edges. Sharp boundaries only where real temperature gradients exist — not optical artifacts.
04
Physics Priors
Emissivity correction, radiative transfer consistency, and surface energy balance constraints keep outputs physically meaningful.
05
Quantitative Metrics
Per-image PSNR, SSIM, and RMSE (Kelvin) computed instantly. Edge alignment score and temperature deviation across land-cover classes.
06
Scalable Processing
Tiled inference with halo padding handles full satellite swaths. TensorRT INT8 export for deployment on edge hardware.
Pipeline Overview

How it works



01
Image Preprocessing
Median filter removes sensor noise. Adaptive histogram equalization (CLAHE) enhances local contrast before any alignment is attempted.
02
Co-Registration
SuperPoint/LoFTR extracts cross-modal feature correspondences. RANSAC-based homography warps TIR onto the optical grid with subpixel accuracy.
03
Feature Extraction
Separate encoders process optical (edges, textures) and thermal (temperature embeddings) streams. Features are kept in their own representation spaces.
04
Guided SR + Decode
Cross-attention fuses the streams. Physics-constrained decoder upsamples with emissivity correction. Output is a full-resolution thermal map.
05
Evaluation
PSNR, SSIM, RMSE (K) measured against reference. Edge sharpness and alignment accuracy scored for qualitative assessment.

Real-World Applications

Where it matters



🏙️
Urban Heat Islands
High-resolution thermal maps reveal fine-scale temperature variation across buildings, roads, and green spaces — critical for urban planning and climate adaptation.
🔥
Wildfire Detection
Spatially precise hotspot maps enable early fire detection and perimeter tracking. Sub-pixel thermal boundaries distinguish flame fronts from warm background.
🌾
Precision Agriculture
Crop stress monitoring through canopy temperature. High-res thermal maps identify irrigation deficits and disease stress at field scale with ±0.5K accuracy.
